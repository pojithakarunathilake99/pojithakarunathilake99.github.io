01 · About

From silicon to cloud

I'm an IoT, embedded and AI engineer with 3+ years of experience building connected hardware that works in the real world. At Complete Cyber I delivered four production-ready IoT/OT products, including devices tested and deployed in trains and train control centre networks.

I write C/C++, Python and Rust for Arm Cortex-M, ESP32 and ARM-based platforms, with FreeRTOS and Yocto-based embedded Linux. I connect devices to AWS and Azure IoT and deploy machine learning at the edge for anomaly detection and predictive maintenance.

I have strong Python expertise: I built the Python/Django backends for CyGard Edge, a cybersecurity handheld device, and CyGard Guardian, an AI/ML intrusion detection system deployed in trains. I also teach AI, Python, IoT, big data and time series to 100+ students.

Security runs through everything I build — secure boot, system hardening and firmware analysis — backed by a BSc in Computer Security and an MSc in Artificial Intelligence (Distinction).
